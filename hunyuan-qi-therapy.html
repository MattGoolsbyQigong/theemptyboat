What is it

Hunyuan Qi Therapy

Hunyuan Qi Therapy is a practice rooted in Zhineng Qigong Science — one of the most sophisticated qigong lineages in modern China, developed by Professor Dr. Pang Ming in the 1980s. It works with Qi, the vital energy that flows through all living things, to support physical healing, emotional balance, and mental clarity.

Unlike approaches that only address symptoms, HYQT works at the root. It combines the therapist's directed energy work with practical movement, meditation, and breathing methods that become part of your own daily life.

Think of it as recharging your body's internal battery — and learning how to keep it full.

Common questions

FAQ

Do I need to believe in Qi for this to work?
No. Many people arrive with skepticism and still experience real results. Openness is more useful than belief — the practice does the rest.
Is this a replacement for medical treatment?
No. HYQT works best alongside conventional medicine, not instead of it. Many people find it enhances the effects of other treatments. Always continue working with your doctor.
How does a remote session work?
Online sessions are just as effective as in-person ones. Qi is not limited by distance. We connect via video and the session follows the same structure.
How many sessions will I need?
This varies by person and condition. Some notice shifts after one session; others benefit from ongoing practice. We will discuss what makes sense for you in the intro call.
How should I prepare?
Nothing special is needed. Come as you are. Wear comfortable clothing, find a quiet space if joining remotely, and bring any intentions or questions you have.
