Hunyuan Qi Therapy

混元氣療法

HYQT is an advanced system of qi-field healing emerging from the Zhineng Qigong tradition — one of the most sophisticated Qigong lineages in modern China. Rather than treating symptoms in isolation, HYQT works directly with the organizing intelligence of the universal qi field, creating the conditions for deep, restorative healing.

Sessions can be conducted in-person or remotely. Qi is not bound by space. The same coherent field that organizes a cell organizes a room, a relationship, and life itself.
